About Mahim Project

A focused infrastructure specialist committed to precision construction and reliable delivery.

Who We Are

Built to Build India's Infrastructure

Mahim Project Solutions is an engineering, highway infrastructure, project consultancy, and project execution company established in 2025, delivering across South India and beyond. We were founded with a clear focus: to deliver precision consultancy and construction on time, every time.

Our expertise spans the complete project lifecycle — highway & road project consultancy, NSV survey, FWD and NDT testing, and MBIU supervision on the advisory side; and RE panel casting and erection, box culverts, underpasses, minor bridges, roadside drains, and road formation on the execution side. We work for NHAI contractors, state PWD departments, and EPC companies across Tamil Nadu and Pan India.

"We bring the focus of a specialist and the reliability of a trusted partner to every project we take on."

Though a young company, our team brings deep experience from years working on major infrastructure projects. We apply that knowledge to deliver to the standards that national highway and state highway projects demand.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Precision

Every panel cast, every wall erected — to the tolerances the design demands. We don't cut corners. Precision is our baseline.

Safety First

Every site operates with mandatory PPE, trained riggers, daily toolbox talks, and documented safety checks. Zero LTI is our standing record.

Reliability

We commit to realistic schedules and we keep them. Our clients can plan around our delivery commitments with confidence.

Transparency

Progress reports, site photography, test records, and direct communication — we keep clients fully informed at every stage.

Partnership

We treat our clients as long-term partners. Understanding their goals lets us deliver work that genuinely solves their problem.

Quality Assurance

IS code compliance, third-party lab coordination, and complete QA/QC documentation submitted with every project handover.

Our Journey

Company Timeline

Company Incorporated

Mid 2025
First RE Panel Contract Secured
Awarded our first Reinforced Earth panel erection contract — an RE wall project in Tamil Nadu for an NHAI HAM contractor.
