Our Methodology

End-To-End Execution Framework

How we assure structural integrity, IS code compliance, and schedule control across all sites.

01

Survey & Testing

Conducting comprehensive NSV surveys, FWD, and non-destructive testing to establish exact ground & structural parameters.

02

Design & Planning

Analyzing structural chainages, foundation soil capacity, level pad design, and casting schedules to prevent site delays.

03

Casting & Quality QA

Precision panel casting using dedicated moulds, standardized concrete mix, strict curing, and batch-wise testing.

04

Site Erection & Handover

Mobilizing specialized hydra cranes and certified riggers for safe, accurate alignment and zero-incident execution.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What scope does your RE panel service cover?

We handle complete turnkey solutions including casting yard setup, Mould fabrication, panel casting, QA curing, low-bed logistics transport, PCC level pad creation, and crane erection on site.

How fast can your teams mobilize equipment?

Because we own our machinery—including Escorts Hydra cranes, mobile cranes, and low-bed trailers—we can mobilize equipment and site teams within 48 to 72 hours of project sign-off.

Do you provide testing and survey documentation?

Yes. All NSV surveys, FWD pavement deflection tests, and non-destructive structural tests come complete with formal QA/QC reports formatted to NHAI and MORTH specifications.
